Viewing Permission Reports
==========================

This section describes how to view the resource permission policies and policy details.

Prerequisites
-------------

The permission policy has been configured. For details on how to configure a permission policy, see :ref:`Configuring Resource Permissions <dataartsstudio_01_1006>`.

Viewing the Details of a Policy
-------------------------------

#. On the DataArts Studio console, locate a workspace and click **DataArts Security**.
#. Choose **Permission Reports** from the left navigation bar.
#. Choose an MRS cluster (Ranger) and select a service to view its policies and policy details.

   -  Advanced search:

      When viewing a report, you can search for policies by cluster, policy name, username, user group, policy type, or policy status. You only need to click **Advanced Search** in the upper right corner of the **Permission Reports** page to display the search box.


      .. figure:: /_static/images/en-us_image_0000002234081948.png
         :alt: **Figure 1** Advanced search

         **Figure 1** Advanced search

   -  Policy status filtering:

      In the policy list of a service, you can click |image1| in the **Policy Status** column to filter the policies to be viewed.
